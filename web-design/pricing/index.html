Pricing FAQ

The money questions.

Is the website really free?

Yes. Build, hosting and basic maintenance cost nothing, forever. There's no trial and nothing to pay to get started. You pay for your domain at cost, and for changes only if you choose Standard or bigger projects.

What counts as a small change?

Text edits, swapping an image, adding a link, or a minor layout tweak. They're $20–$40 AUD each on Standard, and included while you're on WebCare+.

What counts as bigger work?

New pages, rebuilds, booking or payment systems, integrations, custom code, and online stores. You get a price before anything starts — bigger work is $99 base + $39/page on WebCare+, or $200 base + $100/page on Standard. A complete restyle is quoted first, then half price on WebCare+.

What happens if I cancel WebCare+?

There's a 3-month minimum, then you can cancel any time. The site keeps running free, exactly like Standard — changes just move to the Standard rates.

Who pays for the domain?

You do, at cost, and it's registered in your name. Same for any third-party services a project needs. I don't mark them up.

Do the prices ever change?

The rates on this page are current as of September 2026. The rates here are what I charge — if they change, this page changes first.

Is there a lock-in?

Standard has no term at all, because nothing is charged. WebCare+ has a 3-month minimum; after that it's month to month.
