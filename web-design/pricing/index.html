Pricing FAQ

The money questions.

Is the website really free?

Yes. Build, hosting and basic maintenance cost nothing, with no lock-in. There's no trial and nothing to pay to get started. You pay for your domain at cost, and for changes only if you choose Standard or bigger projects.

What counts as a small change?

Text edits, swapping an image, adding a link, a colour or font tweak, or a minor layout fix. They're $40 AUD flat each on Standard.

What's included in WebCare+?

Five small changes a month from day one, $10 for each extra, no rollover. Daily monitoring and priority support also start day one. The bigger-work rate ($99 base + $39/page) and the half-price restyle unlock after 3 continuous months — or from day one if you join within 10 days of go-live. Cancel and rejoin and the three months start again.

What counts as bigger work?

New pages, rebuilds, booking or payment systems, integrations, custom code, and online stores. You get a price before anything starts — $200 base + $100/page on Standard, or $99 base + $39/page on WebCare+ once you're eligible (3 continuous months, or from day one if you join within 10 days of go-live). A complete restyle is quoted first, then half price on WebCare+ under the same rule.

What happens if I cancel WebCare+?

There's a 3-month minimum, then you can cancel any time. The site keeps running free, exactly like Standard — changes just move to the Standard rates.

What does priority support actually mean?

Sites break in quiet ways — a contact form stops sending, a page looks wrong on a new phone. On Standard I check your site every two weeks. On WebCare+ it's every day and fixes come first, so problems get caught before they cost you enquiries.

Who pays for the domain?

You do, at cost, and it's registered in your name. Same for any third-party services a project needs. I don't mark them up.

Do the prices ever change?

The rates on this page are current as of September 2026. They're fixed — if they change, this page changes first.

Is there a lock-in?

Standard has no term at all, because nothing is charged. WebCare+ has a 3-month minimum; after that it's month to month.
