FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What does the $199 website include?

One responsive service page using your text and photos, contact links, agreed domain connection and handover, and one consolidated minor revision batch up to 30 minutes. Additional pages, forms, writing and custom work need a separate quote.

Is the preview free?

A requested preview is free and has no obligation. Preview acceptance alone starts neither a build contract nor care billing. The written scope and proposal must be accepted before a bank-transfer invoice is issued.

How do I request a website?

Use the central Tradsiee enquiry process. I review your requirements and reply in writing with scope and feasibility before any agreement. No calls or account are required. The video product is separate and optional.

How do payments work?

$99.50 deposit after written scope acceptance and before development, then $99.50 after completed-site acceptance and before authorised launch or source handover. Bank transfer only, with private invoice details. No payment or automatic work begins when you enquire.

Standard or WebCare+?

Standard is $15/month for hosting and agreed basic technical care; small changes are $40 each. Optional WebCare+ is $79/month total including hosting, up to two small changes and 30 minutes total per billing month. Each edit takes up to 15 minutes including QA and deployment. Extra care edits are $30 with approval. The monthly prices are alternatives.

What if I join or cancel care later?

The same entitlement applies from service commencement whenever you join. Written cancellation stops renewal at the paid-period end. Continued Standard hosting needs a separately accepted agreement; existing free-hosting terms remain protected. No initial three-month minimum or unused allowance rollover.

How long does a website take?

Timing is confirmed in the written proposal after scope, supplied content, rights and account access are reviewed. A requested preview is not a launch commitment.

What do you need from me?

Your business, services, area, goals and return email at enquiry stage. Before production I need accurate text, usable photos and the necessary rights and agreed access. No passwords or sensitive information belong in the enquiry.

What support is available?

Written support is arranged within Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Melbourne time, excluding Victorian public holidays. New care sales await operational verification. Monitoring, backups, recovery and response-time guarantees are not advertised as verified.

Who owns the site?

The written proposal records commissioned website rights, source/export assets and third-party licence exceptions. Optional care does not add an export fee. Domains stay in the client’s own account, and migration work is quoted separately.
