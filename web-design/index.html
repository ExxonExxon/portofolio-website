FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does the free website include?

The full build, hosting, and basic maintenance — free forever. You pay for the domain and any third-party services, and you only pay me if you want changes handled or bigger work done later.

Why is it free?

I'm 15 and building a portfolio of real client sites. Free builds get me the thing I can't buy: proof, reviews, and referrals. I'd rather do good work for real businesses now than talk about myself later.

Do I need to use Tradsiee?

No. This offer stands on its own. If you also want video leads, that's a separate product at tradsiee.com.

How long does a website take?

The first version usually takes a few days. Most sites are live within a week or two — it depends on how quickly the text and photos come together.

What do you need from me?

Your services, the suburbs you work in, and any photos or reviews you already have. If photos are thin we work with what's there and improve them over time.

Standard or WebCare+?

Start free with Standard if you're happy to send me a message when something needs changing. Pick WebCare+ if you'd rather just text me the change and forget it. The website is the same either way.

Do you only build for tradies?

That's the focus — plumbers, sparkies, chippies, tilers and landscapers. If you run a different local business, ask anyway.

Are you based in Melbourne?

Yes, and most of my work is around Melbourne. The whole process runs online, so distance isn't a problem if you're elsewhere in Australia.
