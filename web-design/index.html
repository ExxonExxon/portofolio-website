What does the $199 website include?

Two responsive pages: a service page and a contact page using your text and photos, contact links, the agreed contact method, domain connection and handover, plus one consolidated minor revision batch up to half an hour across both pages. Hosted with us on separately accepted Standard or WebCare+. Extra pages are $100 each, agreed first. Custom forms, writing and other custom work need a separate quote.

What does the $499 website-only offer include?

Four responsive pages using your supplied content, one revision batch up to half an hour, site files and build instructions. Extra pages are $100 each, agreed first. You supply and run your own hosting and domain. Pay a $50 deposit and $449 before handover. Delivery target: 3 to 5 working days once content and access are complete. No monitoring, backups, uptime promise, ongoing content updates, hosting help or care plan. We fix errors in our original work at no charge. New requests and problems caused by later changes are assessed separately, without limiting your legal rights. Later changes are $80 an hour with a one hour minimum. Bank transfer only; no GST added, the business is not GST registered.

Standard or WebCare+?

Standard is $15/month for hosting and agreed basic technical care, one free small change a year up to 15 minutes, and six free tiny fixes a year, each under 5 minutes, text, hours or phone only, next business day. Extra small changes are $35 each; photo swaps are free inside a paid change. It includes an annual 20 minute health check, checklist only. Usual turnaround is 3 business days; monitoring every 14 days is subject to readiness. Optional WebCare+ is $79/month total including hosting, 45 minutes per month with no request cap, one month rollover capped at 90 minutes, tiny fixes and photo swaps inside the monthly time, and extra time at $25 per 15 minutes with approval. Usual turnaround is 2 business days, with an annual health check and short written report. The monthly prices are alternatives; new care sales remain subject to readiness.

What if I join or cancel care later?

The same entitlement applies from service commencement whenever you join. Written cancellation stops renewal at the paid-period end. Continued Standard hosting needs a separately accepted agreement. There is no initial three-month minimum. WebCare+ allows one month rollover capped at 90 minutes. Prices and allowances may change for existing customers too, with at least 30 days written notice, from the next billing period, never backdated. The notice explains what changes and when. The accepted build price is fixed, including any unpaid balance. Extra work needs a separately accepted quote. Changes to an accepted build agreement need both parties in writing. Only monthly changes allowed in the proposal take effect on notice, from the next billing period after notice. You may cancel before the change starts with no exit fee and no new charge. Cancelling hosting does not depend on buying migration help. Separate accepted legacy agreements keep their terms.
