How It Works

From first call to live.

  1. 1

    We have a quick chat

    Tell me about the business, the jobs you want more of, and the sites you like. I'll ask about your services, the suburbs you work in, and what you already have: photos, reviews, a logo. Ten minutes on the phone and I know what to build.

  2. 2

    I build it

    You get a real link to look at within days. No endless mockup rounds. It's built for phones first, loads fast, and is set up so Google can tell what you do and where you work. The words stay plain, the way customers search.

    You tell me what to change: wording, photos, the order of things. We go back and forth until it feels right, then it goes live on your own domain with your number and contact form ready.

    Hosting and basic maintenance stay free, and I watch the site so problems get caught early. If you'd rather not think about edits at all, WebCare+ includes five small changes a month: new photos, opening hours, price tweaks.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does the free website include?

The full build, hosting, and basic maintenance, all free with no lock-in. You pay for the domain and any third-party services, and you only pay me if you want changes handled or bigger work done later.

Why is it free?

I'm 15 and I want a portfolio of real client sites. Free builds get me proof, reviews and referrals, which are worth more to me right now than a build fee. I'd rather do good work for real businesses now than talk about myself later.

Do I need to use Tradsiee?

No. This offer stands on its own. If you also want video leads, that's a separate product at tradsiee.com.

How long does a website take?

The first version usually takes a few days. Most sites are live within a week or two, and it depends on how quickly the text and photos come together.

What do you need from me?

Your services, the suburbs you work in, and any photos or reviews you already have. If photos are thin we work with what's there and improve them over time.

Standard or WebCare+?

Start free with Standard if you're happy to pay $40 when something needs changing. Pick WebCare+ if you'd rather text me the change and forget it. Five small changes a month are included, or three a month for the first three months if you join after the first 10 days. The website is the same either way.

What does priority support actually mean?

Sites break in quiet ways: a contact form stops sending, a page looks wrong on a new phone. On Standard I check your site every two weeks. On WebCare+ it's every day and fixes come first, so problems get caught before they cost you enquiries.

What if I join WebCare+ later?

You still get the plan from day one. The only difference is that the first 3 months run at three small changes a month instead of five, and the bigger-work rate and half-price restyle kick in after those 3 continuous months. Join within 10 days of go-live and both start straight away.

Who do you build for?

Mostly tradies and local small businesses around Melbourne: plumbers, tilers, sparkies, cafés, clinics and studios. If you're not sure whether you fit, just ask.

Are you based in Melbourne?

Yes, and most of my work is around Melbourne. The whole process runs online, so distance isn't a problem if you're elsewhere in Australia.
