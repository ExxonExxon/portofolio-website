About

I build things that help people.

I'm Tomas Gorjux, 15, from Melbourne. Born in Lithuania, moved here young. I've always liked making things that help people. These days that means software and websites.

That led to Tradsiee, a video lead platform for Australian tradies. Tradespeople around me were losing hours to quotes that never became jobs, so I built a way for them to get real video leads they can quote without driving out.

I build websites at a lower price to grow my portfolio. Free to build and host if a tradie uses Tradsiee.

Outside of building, I shoot mostly nature and am getting into street photography. See the gallery.
