About

I talk to the people I build for. Then I build.

My name is Tomas Gorjux. I'm 15 years old and I live in Melbourne, Australia. I was born in Lithuania, and my family moved here when I was young. Ever since I was a kid I've enjoyed creating things that help people out, and more recently that's turned into building software and websites.

Because of that, I founded Tradsiee, a video lead generation platform for Australian tradespeople. Instead of chasing quote requests that never become jobs, tradies get real video leads, so they can assess and quote via video without having to drive out there, less time hunting and more time working. I created Tradsiee after I noticed how much time was being lost from tradies around me.

I also make websites for people at a lower price, since I want to grow my portfolio. And if a tradie asks for a website, I'll build it and host it for free, as long as they use Tradsiee.

Aside from building, I have a passion for photography. It's my main hobby, mostly nature, and I've been trying to get into street photography. I'm still learning, and you can see some of my photos in the gallery.
