The terms, in plain words

  • Written scope acceptance comes before an invoice. Preview acceptance alone starts neither a build contract nor care billing. No payment is taken by the enquiry process.
  • One supplied-content edit means a bounded change to existing text/contact details/hours/images/links. New pages, sections, structural/design changes, writing, custom forms, booking/payments, integrations or complex repairs require a quote. Correcting defective work does not consume the client's content allowance or waive remedies.
  • Monthly service, no initial three-month minimum, no ten-day joining advantage, no late-join allowance ramp or rejoin penalty. Same entitlement from service commencement whenever the customer joins. Monthly invoicing in advance from service commencement; no automatic debit.
  • A written cancellation request stops renewal at the end of the paid service period. Care-only cancellation returns a future client to their separately accepted $15 Standard hosting agreement; if they also cancel hosting, agree export/migration handover instead of assuming continued payment. Existing free-hosting customers return to their agreed free Standard, not $15.
  • No routine partial-month change-of-mind refund. Correct erroneous/duplicate charges; refund unearned build amounts after lawful, expressly agreed work charges; preserve statutory and service-failure remedies. Legal review remains outstanding.
  • The domain is registered to the client in their own account; use delegated access where needed, and agree renewal responsibilities. Clients provide accurate material with appropriate rights. Premium assets, licences and external services need prior disclosure and approval.
  • Provide the agreed source/export package without a care-dependent fee, including build instructions and transferrable assets; identify nontransferrable items before acceptance. DNS/email/forms/integration migration labour is quoted first; external hosting is the client's cost. Source rights and third-party licence exceptions are recorded in the written proposal.
  • Business hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Melbourne time, excluding Victorian public holidays. Support scheduling is agreed per request. Readiness checks remain open before new care sales.
  • Illustrations for a $199 build plus 12 hosted months: $379 Standard or $1,147 WebCare+. Launch-month full-build totals: $214 or $278. These examples create no minimum term or annual payment product.
  • Existing accepted website, free-hosting, legacy-price and service commitments remain effective. These new prices apply only to eligible new agreements.
