The terms, in plain words

  • Written scope acceptance comes before an invoice. Preview acceptance alone starts neither a build contract nor care billing. No payment is taken by the enquiry process.
  • One small supplied-content change is a bounded edit to existing text/contact details/hours/images/links, up to 15 minutes including QA and deployment. Extra pages are $100 each on either build route, agreed in writing first. New sections, structural/design changes, writing, custom forms, booking/payments, integrations or complex repairs require a quote. We fix errors in our original work at no charge. New requests and problems caused by later changes are assessed separately, without limiting your legal rights. Corrections do not use content allowances.
  • Monthly service, no initial three-month minimum, no ten-day joining advantage, no late-join allowance ramp or rejoin penalty. Same entitlement from service commencement whenever the customer joins. Monthly invoicing in advance from service commencement; every invoice states its service period, amount and due date. No automatic debit. Before suspension for an overdue undisputed hosting invoice, we email the amount owed and give at least seven days to pay or dispute it. A disputed invoice does not justify deleting a site or withholding paid-for files.
  • A written cancellation request stops renewal at the end of the paid service period. Care-only cancellation returns a future client to their separately accepted $15 Standard hosting agreement; if they also cancel hosting, agree export/migration handover instead of assuming continued payment. Separate accepted legacy agreements keep their cancellation and hosting terms.
  • Before the first recorded build session, cancellation refunds the deposit in full. After work starts, the deposit is held, because the time has been reserved and spent, and you receive a written record of what was done and held. Prepaid but unbuilt extra pages are refunded in full. If Tradsiee cancels or cannot complete the job, refund work not provided and hand over work already paid for, without limiting legal rights. If we elect to stop hosting, we give 60 days written notice, supply paid-for files and refund prepaid hosting not provided. No routine partial-month change-of-mind refund limits these promises or legal remedies.
  • The domain is registered to the client in their own account; use delegated access where needed, and agree renewal responsibilities. Clients provide accurate material with appropriate rights. Premium assets, licences and external services need prior disclosure and approval.
  • Provide the agreed source/export package without a care-dependent fee, including build instructions and transferrable assets; identify nontransferrable items before acceptance. DNS/email/forms/integration migration labour is quoted first; external hosting is the client's cost. On payment of the agreed build price, Tradsiee assigns the copyright it owns in the proposal's custom work, excluding listed reusable and third-party components. You may use and modify reusable parts as part of your site. Third-party and open-source licences remain. A signed assignment is required, issued with Tomas's signature and identifying the custom work and exclusions.
  • Business hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Melbourne time, excluding Victorian public holidays. Usual turnaround is 3 business days on Standard and 2 on WebCare+. Standard tiny fixes are next business day, limited to text, hours or phone, each under 5 minutes. These are service targets, not guarantees. Monitoring every 14 days on Standard is arranged when the care plan starts.
  • Standard includes one free small change a year, up to 15 minutes, and six free tiny fixes a year. Extra small changes are $35 each; photo swaps inside a paid change cost no extra. Its annual 20 minute health check is checklist only. WebCare+ includes 45 minutes a month with no request cap, one month rollover capped at 90 minutes, and tiny fixes inside that time. Extra time is $25 per 15 minutes, approved first. Its annual health check includes a short written report.
  • The $199 build covers a service page and a contact page, hosted with us on separately accepted Standard or WebCare+. Payment is $99.50 deposit and $99.50 balance before authorised launch or handover. The $499 website-only build covers four pages, with a $50 deposit and $449 before handover. You supply and run your own hosting and domain on that route: no monitoring, backups, uptime promise, ongoing content updates, hosting help or care plan. Later changes are $80 an hour, with a one hour minimum. Error corrections and statutory remedies remain separate.
  • Build delivery targets, once content and access are complete: 1 to 2 working days for the $199 build, 3 to 5 for the $499 build. Exact dates and extra-page timing are agreed in writing. Bank transfer only. All prices are AUD; no GST is added because the business is not GST registered.
  • Illustrations for a $199 build plus 12 hosted months: $379 Standard or $1,147 WebCare+. Launch-month full-build totals: $214 or $278. These examples create no minimum term or annual payment product.
  • Prices and allowances may change for existing customers as well as new ones. At least 30 days written notice is given, with changes taking effect from the next billing period after notice, never backdated. The notice explains what changes and when, including old and new prices or allowances. The accepted build price is fixed, including any unpaid balance. Extra work needs a separately accepted quote. Build-agreement changes need both parties in writing; only permitted monthly changes take effect on notice. You may cancel before the change starts with no exit fee and no new charge. Cancelling hosting does not depend on buying migration help. No transfer may reduce your rights without consent. Separate accepted legacy agreements keep their terms until you agree to move.
