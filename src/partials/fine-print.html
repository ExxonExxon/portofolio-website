The terms, in plain words

  • Written scope acceptance comes before an invoice. Preview acceptance alone starts neither a build contract nor care billing. No payment is taken by the enquiry process.
  • One small supplied-content change is a bounded edit to existing text/contact details/hours/images/links, up to 15 minutes including QA and deployment. Extra pages are $100 each on either build route, agreed in writing first. New sections, structural/design changes, writing, custom forms, booking/payments, integrations or complex repairs require a quote. Correcting defective work is free with no time limit, does not consume the client's content allowance and does not waive statutory remedies.
  • Monthly service, no initial three-month minimum, no ten-day joining advantage, no late-join allowance ramp or rejoin penalty. Same entitlement from service commencement whenever the customer joins. Monthly invoicing in advance from service commencement; no automatic debit.
  • A written cancellation request stops renewal at the end of the paid service period. Care-only cancellation returns a future client to their separately accepted $15 Standard hosting agreement; if they also cancel hosting, agree export/migration handover instead of assuming continued payment. Separate accepted legacy agreements keep their cancellation and hosting terms.
  • No routine partial-month change-of-mind refund. Correct erroneous/duplicate charges; refund unearned build amounts after lawful, expressly agreed work charges; preserve statutory and service-failure remedies. Legal review remains outstanding.
  • The domain is registered to the client in their own account; use delegated access where needed, and agree renewal responsibilities. Clients provide accurate material with appropriate rights. Premium assets, licences and external services need prior disclosure and approval.
  • Provide the agreed source/export package without a care-dependent fee, including build instructions and transferrable assets; identify nontransferrable items before acceptance. DNS/email/forms/integration migration labour is quoted first; external hosting is the client's cost. Source rights and third-party licence exceptions are recorded in the written proposal.
  • Business hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Melbourne time, excluding Victorian public holidays. Usual turnaround is 3 business days on Standard and 2 on WebCare+. Standard tiny fixes are next business day, limited to text, hours or phone, each under 5 minutes. These are service targets, not guarantees. Monitoring every 14 days on Standard is subject to readiness checks, which remain open before new care sales.
  • Standard includes one free small change a year, up to 15 minutes, and six free tiny fixes a year. Extra small changes are $35 each; photo swaps inside a paid change cost no extra. Its annual 20 minute health check is checklist only. WebCare+ includes 45 minutes a month with no request cap, one month rollover capped at 90 minutes, and tiny fixes inside that time. Extra time is $25 per 15 minutes, approved first. Its annual health check includes a short written report.
  • The $199 build covers a service page and a contact page, hosted with us on separately accepted Standard or WebCare+. Payment is $99.50 deposit and $99.50 balance before authorised launch or handover. The $499 website-only build covers four pages, with $249.50 deposit and $249.50 before handover. You supply and run your own hosting and domain on that route: no monitoring, backups, uptime promise, ongoing content updates, hosting help or care plan. Later changes are $80 an hour, with a one hour minimum. Defect fixes stay free with no time limit.
  • Build delivery targets, once content and access are complete: 1 to 2 working days for the $199 build, 3 to 5 for the $499 build. Exact dates and extra-page timing are agreed in writing. Bank transfer only. All prices are AUD; no GST is added because the business is not GST registered.
  • Illustrations for a $199 build plus 12 hosted months: $379 Standard or $1,147 WebCare+. Launch-month full-build totals: $214 or $278. These examples create no minimum term or annual payment product.
  • Prices and allowances may change for existing customers as well as new ones. At least 30 days written notice is given, with changes taking effect from the next billing period, never backdated. Work already accepted and paid is not repriced. You may cancel during the notice period without paying the new price and keep your site files. Separate accepted legacy agreements keep their terms until you agree to move.
