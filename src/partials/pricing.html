The fine print, in plain words

  • Free means free: I build the site, host it, and keep it running, with no lock-in.
  • A small change is text, an image, a link, a colour or font tweak, or a small fix. On Standard they're $40 AUD flat each.
  • WebCare+ includes 5 small changes a month from day one. Any extra in the same month is $10 each, and unused changes don't roll over.
  • Bigger work covers new pages, rebuilds, booking or payment systems, integrations, custom code, and online stores. Rates: $200 base + $100 per page on Standard; $99 base + $39 per page on WebCare+.
  • *The WebCare+ bigger-work rate and 50% restyle discount unlock after 3 continuous months on the plan — or from day one if you join within 10 days of go-live. Until then, bigger work is billed at Standard rates. Cancel and rejoin and the 3-month clock restarts.
  • Priority support means WebCare+ problems get fixed first. Daily checks catch them sooner, and fixes don't sit behind Standard work.
  • A complete restyle is quoted first too, then half price on WebCare+ (same 3-month / 10-day rule).
  • WebCare+ has a 3-month minimum. Cancel after that and the site keeps running free, exactly like Standard. Changes move to the Standard rate.
  • Domain, plugins, and other third-party services are registered in your name and paid by you, at cost.
  • You supply the text and photos. I handle the layout and the build.
  • Prices are fixed, and current as of September 2026.
