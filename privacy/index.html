What I collect

  • Messages: the contact form collects your name, email address and message. Netlify Forms processes the submission. Email providers also process messages and replies.
  • Service logs: Netlify may keep IP addresses, device/browser information, requested pages and timestamps for delivery, security and abuse prevention.
  • Analytics: the site loads Google Analytics 4 when your browser reports an Australian time zone. GA4 collects usage information and cookie identifiers. A time-zone check is not a residency or consent check.

How it is used

I use messages to assess and answer enquiries and service logs to operate and protect the site. I use aggregated analytics reports to understand visits, not to identify named visitors. I do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Providers and overseas processing

Netlify is US-based with a global delivery network. It hosts the site, processes contact submissions and keeps service logs. Reading a message does not automatically delete it from Netlify. Google processes analytics on its own infrastructure under Google's privacy policy. Email providers have their own storage and retention practices. This is not an Australia-only setup.

Cookies and tracking

GA4 uses cookies and similar identifiers when it loads. You can adjust browser cookie settings or use Google's analytics opt-out add-on. The site does not currently show a consent banner. Applicable privacy and cookie requirements await Australian lawyer confirmation. This notice is not a claim that disclosure alone supplies legally required consent.

The separate /track/open outreach pixel records its supplied reference, time and user agent in Netlify Blobs. It is not used on ordinary site pages. This disclosure does not authorise outreach or tracking without the required permission.

How long information is kept

Unsuccessful enquiries are reviewed three months after last contact or last meaningful update, whichever is later, then deleted unless a live reason remains, such as a current proposal or unresolved dispute. This is review, not automatic deletion. Agreements and financial records are kept for as long as tax and business law requires. Other client information is reviewed and deleted when no longer needed.

Provider logs, analytics and copies follow provider settings and practices. Current account-specific periods have not been verified; no exact deletion date is promised. Specific legal holds are assessed separately.
